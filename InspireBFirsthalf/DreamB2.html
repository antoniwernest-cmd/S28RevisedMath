Ontario Mathematics Curriculum

Panther F Before After A

Grade 3

B1.2 · B. Number · B1. Number Sense · Whole Numbers

Compare and order whole numbers up to and including 1000, in various contexts.

How this page addresses it: Students use the stable order of whole numbers to identify the number immediately before and immediately after each three-digit number.

C1.4 · C. Algebra · C1. Patterns and Relationships · Patterns

Create and describe patterns to illustrate relationships among whole numbers up to 1000.

How this page addresses it: Students recognize and apply the count-by-one pattern, describing each neighbouring number as one less or one greater than the centre number.

Source: The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 1–8: Mathematics, 2020.
